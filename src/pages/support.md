# Disciplined Club: Support

Last updated 2026-10-21. Start with the answers below, or write to us.

### Using without an account

Choose "continue without an account" on the sign-in screen. Your logs stay on this phone, including after closing the
app. In Settings, "Sign in to sync" opens sign-in; "back to my data" returns without changing your logs. Signing in
syncs the logs with your account, with existing account settings taking priority and schedule and food entries merged.
Suggestions are rule-based until you sign in and allow AI. "Erase data on this phone" in Settings deletes all guest
data after confirmation. Uninstalling or losing your phone can lose guest data: it has no cloud backup before sign-in.

### Signing in

Type your email address in the app and we send you a 6-digit code from noreply@disciplinedclub.com. Type it in the
app within 15 minutes. There is no password. The same email signs you in on every device, with the same data. No code?
Check your spam folder, wait a minute and ask for a new code. Codes can only be requested a few times an hour.

### The 30-day challenge and your streak

The rules are in the app, on the Challenge tab, and the text in the app is the final word. If your streak looks wrong, email us the date, your time zone and a screenshot of the Challenge tab.

### Morning reminder

The app can remind you each morning at 05:30 (local time), before the 06:00 deadline. The reminder is set on your phone.
To turn it off, tap "turn off" on the Challenge tab, or open your phone's Settings, then Notifications, then Disciplined
Club, and switch off Allow Notifications (on iPhone, see [Apple's guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120681)). On
Android the reminder can arrive late unless you allow "Alarms & reminders" for the app in Android settings: when that
applies, the Challenge tab shows a line with an "open settings" button. The rest of the app works without notifications.

### AI features

Nutrition targets, coach tips and weekly plans use AI (currently Anthropic's Claude, through our servers). We ask your
permission before you use them the first time. To withdraw it, open Settings in the app and switch off AI suggestions
in the Privacy section. They stop at once, and the rest of the app keeps working. Suggestions can be wrong and are not
medical advice. Every suggestion has a "report" link under it: it opens a short form in the app, and your report goes
to our team with the suggestion, linked to your account. Use it if one looks wrong. The
[Privacy Policy](https://disciplinedclub.com/privacy) says what is sent and how long we keep it.

### Leaderboard

The leaderboard is optional and starts off. You can turn it on or off on the Challenge tab; joining shows the community
rules and asks you to agree. Signed-in members who joined see your display name (the first name you typed when you set
up the app) and your streak. To report a name or hide a member from your own board, tap "•••" beside their row and
choose a reason (offensive name, harassment or other) or block them; "manage blocked members" undoes a block. Reports go
privately to our team, and you can also email support@disciplinedclub.com. We review reports within 2 business days and can hide
names that break our [Terms of Use](https://disciplinedclub.com/terms).

### Deleting your account

Open the app, tap Settings (top right of the Home tab), scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account. You don't need to
email us. We delete your account and the data linked to it, except what the law requires us to keep. You can't undo it.
Lost your phone, or uninstalled the app? The steps below ("Delete your account") work without the app.

### Videos

The Reset tab shows videos from YouTube by Kim Chiaretti, the coach behind The Disciplined Club. When you open the tab, your phone loads the preview images from
YouTube. When you tap a video, it opens in the YouTube app or on youtube.com, and YouTube's terms and privacy policy
apply there. The [Privacy Policy](https://disciplinedclub.com/privacy) has the details.

### Reporting a bug

Email support@disciplinedclub.com with: what happened and what you expected; the app version (Settings, the Version line under
About); your phone's operating system version and model; a screenshot or screen recording; and when it happened, with
your time zone. Never send passwords. The app also sends crash reports (see the Privacy Policy).

### Privacy and health

Read the [Privacy Policy](https://disciplinedclub.com/privacy). To ask for a copy, correction or deletion of your data, email
support@disciplinedclub.com. Disciplined Club gives general information, not medical advice: ask your doctor before you change
your diet or training.

## Delete your account

**Disciplined Club: delete your account.** Last updated 2026-10-21. This page is for the Disciplined Club app,
published by Founders University. You can delete your account, and the data we hold about you, in the app or without it.

### In the app

1. Open the app and tap "settings" at the top right of the Home tab.
2. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Delete account".
3. Read the note and tap "Delete account" again. It takes effect right away and can't be undone.

### Without the app

If you uninstalled the app or lost your phone, write to support@disciplinedclub.com from the email address you sign in with, with
the subject "Delete my account". We may ask you to confirm that it is you. We reply within 2 business days and finish
within one month at the latest. You can also install the app on a new phone, sign in with the same email address and
delete the account there.

### What we delete

Your profile (first name, goal, time zone and consents), your challenge history, food log, targets, training plan and
logs, schedule items, AI suggestions and AI usage log, the reports you filed about AI suggestions, the community reports and blocks you made, your leaderboard
entry if you had one, and your sign-in account. We delete it from our live database right away.

### What we keep, and for how long

- Copies in server and security logs, which include IP addresses: up to 7 days.
- Crash and error reports at Sentry: 30 days.
- Sign-in code emails at Resend: 30 days.
- AI requests at Anthropic: deleted automatically within 30 days, or up to 2 years if its safety systems flag a request.
- Update requests at Expo: as long as Expo needs them to run its update service.
- Encrypted backups of our database: 30 days, so your data leaves them within 30 days.
- A small encrypted record of your deletion or withdrawal (your internal account ID, the kind of request and the time): 30 days, so a restore after a failure does not bring deleted data back.
- Our emails with you: 12 months after we close your request.
- Records the law requires: as long as the law says.

Deleting the app from your phone does not delete your account.

### Delete only your health data

To keep your account and delete only your health data (check-ins, food, targets, training, body measurements and the
reports you filed about AI suggestions), open Settings in the app, switch off "Health data" and confirm "Withdraw and
delete". Or ask us by email.
