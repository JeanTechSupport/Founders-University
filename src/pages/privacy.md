# Privacy Policy: Disciplined Club app

Effective 2026-10-21

This policy covers the Disciplined Club app. It explains what personal data we collect, why, who else handles
it, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it. Questions? Write to support@disciplinedclub.com.

**In short:** we collect only what the features you use need. We treat your body measurements, meals, training and daily
check-ins as health data, and we use them only with your explicit consent. Nothing goes to an AI service until you agree.
No ads, no tracking, no selling your data. You can delete your account in the app at any time.

## 1. Who we are

Founders University ("we", "us") runs the Disciplined Club app. Our address is Predikherenstraat 10/1, 3700 Tongeren-Borgloon, Belgium, and our registration number is
1032.387.925 (Belgian enterprise number, KBO/BCE). Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we are the "controller" of your personal
data. Write to us at support@disciplinedclub.com. We have not appointed a data protection officer.

## 2. What we collect

**Without an account.** You can choose "continue without an account". Your profile, challenge, food, schedule and
training logs stay on this phone until you sign in. Suggestions come from rules on the phone; no guest health data
is sent to Anthropic and no report is sent to our server. Settings lets you erase the data on this phone, or withdraw
health consent and delete the local health data. Signing in syncs your local logs with that account: saved account
settings win where they already exist, and schedule and food entries are merged when health consent is on. If health
consent is off on the existing account, your local health logs are erased rather than uploaded; schedule entries remain. Uninstalling or losing the phone
can lose unsynced guest data. Crash reports, app update checks and YouTube preview images still use the services
listed below.

Most of this data comes from you: you type it in or tap it. Some comes from your phone (time zone, device model,
operating system version and app version).

## 3. Why we use your data, and our legal basis

Some of this data can reveal information about your health: your weight and height, what you eat, how you train, whether
you drank alcohol or used drugs, and when you wake up. EU law (GDPR Article 9) treats this as health data, so we process it
only with your explicit consent. The app asks for that consent before you save any of it, and without it we cannot offer
the tracking features. You can withdraw it at any time (section 11).

| What we do | Data | Legal basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Create your account, sign you in, keep your data in sync, and run the schedule | Account, profile, schedule | Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): we need it to give you the app |
| Run the challenge, food and macro tracking, and the training log | Check-ins, meals, targets, body measurements, training log | Your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)) and contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Send your data to Anthropic for AI targets, tips and plans | AI inputs (section 4) | Your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a)), asked before your first AI request |
| Read and act on your reports about AI suggestions | AI reports (section 2) | Your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a)), given when you tap Send report on a form that shows what is sent |
| Show your display name and streak on the leaderboard, if it is available | Display name, streak (current and best) | Your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a)), when you switch it on |
| Find and fix crashes, deliver app updates, keep the service secure, stop abuse, and keep AI costs under control | Crash reports, update requests, IP addresses, AI usage log | Our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) in a stable, secure and affordable service. You can object (section 10). |
| Keep encrypted backups, so we can restore your data after a failure | A copy of the data above (section 6, Cloudflare R2) | The same basis as each piece of data in it: a backup is part of keeping that data secure (Art. 32) |
| Answer your messages | Support emails | Our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| Keep records the law requires, handle your rights requests | Varies | Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) |

Giving us your account data is necessary to create an account. Everything else is your choice, but a feature that needs
your data or your consent is not available without it.

## 4. AI features

Three features use AI: the nutrition target calculator, the training coach tip and the next-week training plan. They use
Claude, an AI model from Anthropic.

- **What we send.** For targets: your weight, height, age, sex (if you gave it), activity level, training type and goal.
  For tips and plans: your training split, exercises, weights, sets, reps and ratings. We do not send your name, email
  address or account ID.
- **Young or underweight members.** If you give no age, enter an age under 18, or your weight and height give a body mass index
  under 18.5, the app never sets a calorie deficit for you: your targets never go below maintenance (a goal of gaining
  muscle keeps its surplus), and the app does not ask the AI for them but uses a standard formula. Until you enter an age of 18 or older,
  the app assumes no deficit is safe. Coach tips and training plans still use the AI. Talk to a doctor before you eat
  less.
- **When and how.** Only after you agree on the AI consent screen, and only when you ask for an AI result. The app talks to
  our server, and our server talks to Anthropic. The access key stays on our server.
- **What Anthropic does with it.** Anthropic works for us as a processor. Under its commercial terms, it may not train its
  models on this data. It deletes the data automatically within 30 days, or keeps a request for up to 2 years if its
  automated safety systems flag it as a policy violation. It may process the data in the United States and other regions,
  and stores it in the United States (section 7).
- **Not medical advice.** AI answers can be wrong. They are general fitness and nutrition suggestions, not a diagnosis or
  treatment. Talk to a doctor or a registered dietitian before you make big changes, especially if you have a health
  condition, are pregnant or have had an eating disorder.
- **No automated decisions, and a fallback.** Nothing the AI says changes your access or anything else with
  legal or similar effect. Our server checks each answer against fixed limits. If an answer fails, or if you did not agree
  to AI, the app shows a result from a standard formula and says so.
- **Report a suggestion.** Every AI suggestion has a "report" link. It opens a short form in the app. Nothing is sent
  until you tap Send report. Then we store the suggestion, where it came from and the optional reason you typed, linked
  to your account, so our team can read it and fix what is wrong (section 2). If reporting from the app is not available,
  the form offers to email the report to support@disciplinedclub.com instead.
- **Your choice.** You can turn AI off at any time in Settings (section 11). We then stop sending data to Anthropic.

## 5. The leaderboard

The leaderboard is optional. Your participation starts off; joining shows the community rules and asks you to
agree. Signed-in members can see participants' display names and current and best streaks. They cannot see your
check-ins, meals, weight or training. We also send a random board reference so members can report or block a row;
it is separate from your sign-in account identifier.

Use only the first name you want others to see. You can correct it in Settings. Turning participation off removes
your row when the change reaches the server; other phones refresh their board periodically. You can report a name
or hide someone from your own board using Community controls beside their row. Manage blocked members to undo a
block. Reports and block lists are private to you and our operators, never shared with other members. Reports contain
the reporting account, the board reference, a reason category and the report time. They remain until the reporting
account or reported profile is deleted. Operators may hide a name that breaks the community rules; contact
support@disciplinedclub.com to ask for a review.

## 7. Transfers outside the EU

Our main database is in the European Union. Some of our providers, or their staff, are in the United States and other
countries outside the EU. We protect these transfers as follows. You can get a copy of the clauses by writing to
support@disciplinedclub.com.

- **EU-US Data Privacy Framework.** Sentry, Expo and Cloudflare are certified under it. The European Commission has decided that
  certified US companies protect personal data adequately.
- **Standard Contractual Clauses.** For Anthropic, Supabase and Resend, and as a fallback for Sentry and Expo, the
  contract includes the clauses the European Commission approved for transfers outside the EU. Cloudflare keeps our
  encrypted backups in Western Europe by preference; Cloudflare is a US company, and it has no key to read them.
- **Google** (YouTube) handles its own transfers under its privacy policy.

## 9. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app: open Settings (top right of the Home tab), scroll to the bottom and tap Delete
account. It sits in a card of its own, below the About section. You confirm once; completion is shown after our server confirms deletion. If the request cannot finish, the app tells you to retry.
Deleting the app from your phone does not delete your account. When you delete your account:

- We delete your profile and everything you saved from our live database, including your leaderboard entry, your AI usage
  log and your reports about AI suggestions, and then your sign-in account.
- Our encrypted backups still hold a copy until they expire, at most 30 days later. We use a backup only to recover
  from a failure of our database.
- We keep the limited encrypted recovery record in section 2 for up to 30 days so a recovery does not bring your deleted data back. This is separate from your deleted sign-in account.
- Copies in logs, crash reports and at Anthropic expire on the schedule in section 8. Support emails stay for the time in
  section 8, and records the law requires stay as long as the law says.

You can also delete your account without the app by following the steps at https://disciplinedclub.com/delete-account,
or ask us to delete your data by writing to support@disciplinedclub.com from the email address linked to your account.

## 10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have these rights:

- **Access:** get a copy of your data and details about how we use it.
- **Correction:** have wrong data fixed. You can edit most of your data in the app.
- **Deletion:** have your data deleted. You can delete your account yourself (section 9).
- **Restriction:** ask us to pause using your data, for example while we check a complaint.
- **Portability:** get the data you gave us in a machine-readable format, or have it sent to another provider.
- **Objection:** object to processing based on our legitimate interests (section 3). We stop unless we show a compelling reason.
- **Withdrawing consent:** see section 11.
- **No solely automated decisions:** we make none with legal or similarly significant effects.

To use a right, write to support@disciplinedclub.com from the email address linked to your account. We may ask you to confirm who you
are. We answer within one month, and for a complex request we can extend that by two months and tell you why. It is free,
unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive.

## 11. Withdrawing your consent

You can withdraw any consent at any time, and it is as easy as giving it. In the app, go to Settings, then the Privacy section:

- **Health data:** we stop processing it and delete it, with your reports about AI suggestions. The tracking features stop working until you agree again.
- **AI suggestions:** we stop sending data to Anthropic and delete the AI reports already held for you. Results already created stay in your account until you delete them.
- **Show me on the leaderboard** (only when the leaderboard is available): your entry disappears right away.
  **Notifications:** change this in your phone's settings, under Notifications.

If the server cannot confirm a withdrawal, the app keeps it pending and shows that it is unfinished. We keep the limited encrypted recovery record described in sections 2 and 8 to preserve a completed choice after a failure.

Withdrawing does not affect processing that happened before. You can also write to support@disciplinedclub.com.

## 12. Complaints

If you think we handle your data unlawfully, you can complain to the data protection authority. In Belgium, that is
the Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données, https://www.dataprotectionauthority.be/citizen/actions/lodge-a-complaint).
You can also complain to the authority in the EU country where you live or work. We would like the chance to fix a problem
first, so please write to us as well.

## 13. Minimum age

The app is for people aged 16 and older. You must be at least 16 to use it. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone
younger, and if we learn that someone under 16 has an account, we delete it. We follow the EU General Data Protection
Regulation, including its rules on young people's data, for every member. If you are 16 or 17, the AI suggestions come
from an AI, never from a person, and if you give no age or enter an age under 18 the app never sets a calorie deficit for you (section 4).

## 14. Security

The app and our servers talk over encrypted connections, and our hosting provider encrypts stored data. Database access
rules let each account read only its own data, and the leaderboard (when it is available) shows only what section 5 says.
The AI access key stays on our server. There are no passwords to leak: you sign in with a one-time code we email you. No
system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your rights at risk, we tell you and the regulator as the law requires.
